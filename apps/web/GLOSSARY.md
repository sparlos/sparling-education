# Marketing site

The public marketing website. It explains the product to each audience and routes visitors to the page meant for them.

## Language

### People

**Teacher**:
A person who runs a class in the platform and turns its games on. The product term everywhere outside marketing audience labels.
_Avoid_: Instructor, educator (when meaning the individual product user)

**Educator**:
The marketing audience for the educators page: teachers plus the school and board staff who approve or buy the platform (principals, board IT and privacy officers).
_Avoid_: Using it as a synonym for Teacher in product copy

**Board**:
A school board acting as the buyer of a board licence.
_Avoid_: District, school district

**Parent**:
A parent or guardian of a student; the audience for the parents page.
_Avoid_: Guardian (as a separate term), family

**Student**:
A child in a class who plays the games. The marketing site doesn't address students directly.
_Avoid_: Player, kid, user

### Site

**Audience**:
One of the groups the site speaks to (Educator, Parent, Student), each with its own page and accent colour.
_Avoid_: Persona, segment

**Role chooser**:
The row of "I'm a teacher / parent / student" cards on the home page that sends each visitor to their audience page.
_Avoid_: Gate, identity picker

**Pilot**:
The pre-launch programme where teachers use the platform early, free, in exchange for feedback. The main call to action for Educators.
_Avoid_: Beta, early access, waitlist

**Typical learning games**:
The unnamed group of competing products the platform is compared against. Competitors are never named on the site.
_Avoid_: Competitors, other games, naming specific products
