# Company site

The public one-page site for Sparling Education, the company. It tells visitors who the company is and points them to its products.

## Language

**Company**:
Sparling Education, the business that makes the platform.
_Avoid_: Brand, studio

**Product**:
Something the company makes and sells, with its own name, look and site. The platform is the first product.
_Avoid_: App (when meaning the whole offering), title

**Company site**:
This site. Speaks for the company and features its products.
_Avoid_: Corporate site, parent site, main site

**Product site**:
A product's own marketing site, which the company site links to. For the platform, it's the marketing site.
_Avoid_: Marketing site (when it's unclear which site is meant)
