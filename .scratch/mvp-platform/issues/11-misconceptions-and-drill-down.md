# 11: Misconception strip and drill-down

**What to build:** Above the grid, the teacher sees the misconceptions showing up in their class, ordered by how many students showed each, and opens one to see who. Opening a cell shows that student's evidence for that expectation. Opening a play shows its attempts in order.

**Blocked by:** 10

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The misconception strip lists the misconceptions revealed in recent plays, each with its description and the number of students who showed it. Opening one lists those students
- [ ] The cell view shows the games, tiers, misconceptions and change over time for that student and expectation
- [ ] The play view shows each task's attempts, retries, help used and how the play ended
- [ ] Every view is reachable in one tap from the one above it, and back
- [ ] Integration tests cover the strip and drill-down data. Rendered-app tests cover moving from the strip and from a cell down to a play
