# Every game has a journey

Status: ready-for-human

The tasks of one play lead to something: see **Journey** in `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, and the Journey section in `.claude/skills/design-game/SPEC-TEMPLATE.md`. Cargo Line has its section. Its climb is the example the others follow.

## What the founder decided (2026-11-02)

- A journey lasts one play and starts fresh the next time. Every student gets the payoff in one sitting.
- Three parts, all visible in the game's own world: the goal from the start, closer with each task, a big moment at the end. It either goes somewhere or builds something.
- The ending is the same for every student, however much help they had.
- Nothing is kept after the play. Keepsakes earned over repeated plays are a separate idea: `.scratch/keepsakes/spec.md`.
- Between tasks: at most 4 seconds, with no voice line, nothing to tap and no skip. "It needs to be short enough." The ending is about 5 seconds, with a voice line.
- Rough versions include the journey, so the founder judges a whole play.
