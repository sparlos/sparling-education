# Keepsakes earned over repeated plays

Status: needs-triage

A stub to flesh out in its own design session. Nothing here is decided.

## The idea

A student earns a game's keepsake over repeated plays, instead of getting it the first time they play. Pieces of it could unlock as they play. (Founder's notes, 2026-11-02.)

Since every play of a game now leads to something (Cargo Line's summit, a finished gem, a treasure), each finish could add a piece. For example, each gem Maya finishes in Crystal Machine adds a piece to her keepsake, and her fifth gem completes it.

That's why this was left out of that work: in it, nothing from a play's ending stays with the student. Reworking keepsakes changes the locker and what the server saves.

## Open questions

- **How do we stop students spamming their way to a keepsake?** The founder suggested limiting it to one a day, but DESIGN.md principle 6 rules out daily rewards, because they favour students who can play at home.
- **Does a piece come from playing, or from finishing?** Today a keepsake comes from playing, however the play goes, and records where a student has been, never how well they did. Tying it to finishing would leave behind a student who always runs out of class time.
- **Does the glossary's Keepsake change?** It says "one for each game they've played" and "playing a game gives it". It also says a keepsake isn't a reward. Is one earned over many plays still that?
- **What do students see before it's complete?** And what about students who already have a whole keepsake?
