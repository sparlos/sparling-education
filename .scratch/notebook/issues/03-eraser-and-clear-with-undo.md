# 03: Eraser and clear-with-Undo

**What to build:** a student can fix their working. An eraser removes what they rub over, and a clear button empties the page with one tap and no "Are you sure?". The clear button then turns into Undo until they write again.

**Blocked by:** 02: Notebook over Cargo Line, with a pen

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The student can switch between the pen and the eraser, and can tell which one is active
- [ ] The eraser removes only the marks it touches
- [ ] One tap on clear empties the page with no confirmation
- [ ] After a clear, the button is Undo, and tapping it brings the working back exactly
- [ ] Writing after a clear turns Undo back into clear, and the cleared working can't be brought back
- [ ] A new task empties the page and leaves no Undo for the previous task's working
- [ ] The controls meet target sizes in headless Chrome: 48 px minimum, 64 px for Grades 1–2
- [ ] The controls can be understood without reading, and any new words follow the user-facing content rules
